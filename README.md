## Hi there, I'm Derek Lein 👋

I began my journey into the world of data in 2026. Originally from California, I graduated Chapman University in 2018, where I studied both Film Production and Business Entrepreneurship. After working in the film industry for a decade as an assistant editor, I decided I needed a change and began learning as much as I can about data. I found numerous parallels between the two seemingly disparate fields, with both filmmaking and data visualization using visuals and technical craft to communicate insights to an audience. I now work as a Data Analytics and Engineering Consultant for The Information Lab US, based in New York City.

I will be hosting my personal projects here on Github while I further practice my coding and data visualization skills. Check out my pinned projects below or check out my Tableau Public profile here: [Tableau Public Profile](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/derek.lein/vizzes)
